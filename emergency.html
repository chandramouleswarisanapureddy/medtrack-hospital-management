Emergency Rescheduling Protocol

When an administrator reports an emergency event: 1) All matching confirmed appointments are marked Cancelled - Emergency. 2) The engine scans available schedule slots within the next 7 days (Priority 1: Same Doctor; Priority 2: Alternative Doctor in same Department & Branch). 3) A replacement appointment is created and linked to the original. 4) An In-App notification with old vs new date, doctor, branch, and token is delivered immediately to the patient.
